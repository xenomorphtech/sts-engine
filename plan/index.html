Cross-cutting findings

  • Fidelity The engine does not yet play real A20. Missing: A20 double boss, A11 two potion slots, Time Eater / Nemesis / Reptomancer / Spire Growth / Writhing Mass (silently replaced by Hexaghost / Gremlin Nob / Cultist), several A4/A7/A8/A9/A19 HP-and-moveset tiers. Act 4 is unreachable (win = Act 3), so all key-chasing is live dead weight.
  • A20 death profile (500 seeds): Act 1 boss 32% of runs (Hexaghost 15%!), Act 2 boss 21%, hallways/elites ~27%. The A0-tuned policy under-blocks scripted spikes and enters bosses too poor.
  • Two shared mechanisms cover most combat losses: the scripted-spike table (Hexaghost Divider, Automaton beam, Heart cycle, Giant Head, Collector debuff, Awakened echo) and the status-gain penalty (Wounds/Dazed/Burns invisible today).
  • Structure by hand, magnitudes by ES. Every report converged on the same doctrine, backed by the ledger: hand-set values regress, hard gates fail, but hand-written structure (affinities, spike schedules, DP shape) with near-neutral defaults + ES-tuned scales wins. All additions must be default-preserving.
  • Eval hygiene: the current best-selection argmaxes repeated fixed validation cohorts (~1.5–2pp inflation). Switch to tail-averaged bakes, fresh per-checkpoint sources, McNemar-paired accepts, 10k honest cohorts.
