Critical content gaps

  • Missing Act 3 encounters: no Time Eater, Nemesis, Reptomancer, Spire Growth, Writhing Mass. Fallbacks silently substitute Hexaghost / Gremlin Nob / Cultist. Confirmed: zero floor-47 "Time Eater" losses in 500 A20 runs. ~1/3 of Act 3 boss rolls and ~2/3 of Act 3 elite rolls are fought against trivial stand-ins.
  • Act 4 is unreachable: begin_next_act sets done = true on the Beyond→Ending transition unconditionally. "Win" = completing Act 3. Therefore all key-chasing (ruby recall stealing the pre-boss rest, sapphire over a chest relic, emerald +70 elite bias) is live dead weight at every ascension.
  • The measured 11.0% "A20" is an overestimate of true A20; an ES run today partially optimizes against phantom encounters.

Biggest risks / unknowns

  • Fidelity gap dominates. Every A20 number today is inflated; re-run the ES after each engine fix.
  • Act 4 semantics need a deliberate decision before A20 tuning (key logic interacts with double-boss prep).
  • ES signal sparsity at ~11% wins: use heavier floor weight early (the floor-15 wall is where floor signal is dense), possibly 1000-seed cohorts.
  • Verify from decompile before coding: Awakened A19 Curiosity 2 / A9 320 HP, Automaton A19 pattern, exact double-boss reward/no-heal sequence.
  • A20 leans harder on the frost wall than the A0 damage-forward tuning; if scalars can't express it, a "build a block wall" DeckTask is a hand-written addition the ES cannot invent.
