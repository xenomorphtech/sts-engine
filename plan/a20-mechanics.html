A20 mechanics delta & engine support status

The original 500-seed profile was A0 = 64.2% (floor 42.96) vs pre-audit A20 = 11.0% (floor 24.57), with 32% of all runs dying at the Act-1 boss. The audited fidelity baseline was 0.1% / floor 21.77; after final fidelity corrections, the accepted policy sequence reaches 0.2% / 22.28 on source 20260822 and 0.4% / 22.46 on fresh source 20260823.

Critical content gaps

  • ✅ Formerly missing Act 3 encounters are implemented: Time Eater, Spire Growth, Nemesis, Reptomancer, and Writhing Mass now use their decompiled HP, moves, powers, and summon/debuff lifecycles instead of Gremlin Nob / Cultist stand-ins.
  • Act 4 is unreachable: begin_next_act sets done = true on the Beyond→Ending transition unconditionally. "Win" = completing Act 3. The HTN now gates key-chasing while that remains true, so it no longer spends a campfire, chest relic, or elite-routing premium on dead keys.
  • The audited Act-3 campaign is now a viable A20 policy target. Act 4 is still a separate fidelity project and is deliberately excluded from this win condition.

Biggest risks / unknowns

  • Act-3 fidelity corrections sharply lowered the baseline. Preserve this audited engine while policy work proceeds; treat any future engine correction as a re-baseline event.
  • Act 4 semantics need a deliberate decision before A20 tuning (key logic interacts with double-boss prep).
  • Win signal remains sparse at 0.2–0.5%: use 1,000-seed paired cohorts and heavier floor weight until policy recovery makes win deltas measurable.
  • The Awakened A19/A9, Automaton A19/A9/A4, and exact double-boss reward/no-heal sequences are verified and implemented.
  • A20 leans harder on the frost wall than the A0 damage-forward tuning; if scalars can't express it, a "build a block wall" DeckTask is a hand-written addition the ES cannot invent.
