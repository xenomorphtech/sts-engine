RL & optimization roadmap

From the phase-3 ES (~63% A0, still +1pp/20 gens) to 70%+ A0 and an A20 campaign. Keep what phase 3 got right: common random numbers within a generation, mirrored sampling, pure recombination, fresh cohort per generation.

ES v4 design

  1. Per-dimension step sizes (SNES-style, not full CMA). Full-λ signed rank utilities (bottom half negative) instead of top-4-only; mean update via utilities; log-σ update log σ_d += η_σ · Σ u_i (ε²_{i,d} − 1) with η_σ ≈ 0.06. Replaces frozen hand-set SPEC sigmas — the ~1pp/20gens crawl is the classic signature of per-dim scale mismatch under one global multiplier.
  2. Global sigma schedule: validation-slope controller (hold while 12-gen moving average rises ≥ ~8 wins/2000; step 0.6 → 0.45 → 0.3 on stall; floor ~0.25 where eval noise dominates).
  3. Anneal the floor weight: FLOOR_WEIGHT = max(0.5, 6·(1 − winrate)) — nearly redundant at 62% wins, crucial at <15% (the A20 warm start), where floor carries most of the gradient. Optional dense term: mean boss-entry HP fraction (the measured win/loss discriminator) at weight ~0.3.
  4. Shift budget from seeds to directions: λ=24 (12 mirrored pairs) × 250 seeds = 6,000 sims/gen. Paired-difference variance scales with the seed-flip rate, not binomial win-count variance — 250 shared seeds retain the ranking floor while broadening direction coverage.
  5. Bake protocol: average the last k=8 generation means, never argmax over validations (the current best argmax over ~27 noisy fixed-cohort validations inflates ~1.5–2pp — the live trap). Honest number from a never-touched cohort at bake time.
  6. A20: warm-start from A0 params (hundreds of gens of transferable knowledge), reset sigma to 1.0 for ~30 gens, high floor weight. After promotion, use the constraint-tested A20 bake as the shared A0/A20 default; retain params_default.json only as an explicit legacy regression baseline through STS_HTN_PARAMS.

A20 conversion campaign (live)

ES v4 trainer implemented: tools/opt_params4.py now defaults to A20, warm-starts from the A0 bake until an A20 bake exists, and uses 24 mirrored candidates × 250 common-random-number seeds. It implements full-population signed rank utilities, SNES per-dimension sigmas, the 30-generation A20 exploration hold plus validation-slope global schedule, dynamic floor weight, fresh checkpoint cohorts, resumable staged-dimension activation, and one-candidate-per-machine SSH fan-out. The engine summary exposes exact A20 second-boss entries and clears, Heart entry HP, the raw sum of remaining late-boss HP, normalized damage completion, and bounded combat stalemates. These turn sparse late-boss visits into rank signal while keeping “entered,” “cleared,” and “won the run” unambiguous.

The completed conversion state is tools/opt_state_a20_v4.json; the broadened reach state is tools/opt_state_a20_reach_v4.json, and the bake target remains tools/params_a20.json. There is deliberately no best checkpoint field: the bake is the component-wise average of the last eight generation means, and its honest cohort is recorded with an exact paired McNemar result against that phase's starting bake. New parameter paths can be added to the starting JSON during a resume and activated cumulatively with --stage-file. Reach weight, boss-gauntlet path, and gauntlet weight are persisted in the resume state; exposing batches A–D in Rust remains separate work.

Curriculum design

  1. Tier 1 — boss-weighted cohorts (no engine changes): tabulate conditional win rate by boss from 10k-seed fixtures; build generation cohorts over-weighting the worst boss ~2×. Structural (fixed property of the seed), hence stationary — no cohort adaptation.
  2. Tier 2 — boss-gauntlet fitness (implemented with replayable prefixes): run the incumbent to the exact A20 second-boss predicate, store the deterministic action prefix, replay it to reconstruct the Game, then evaluate candidate combat behavior on the fight alone (clear + normalized damage + remaining HP). The current 217-fight gauntlet replays in about five seconds on 12 local workers, providing far denser conversion signal than 300 full runs. Staged EM loop: use weight 0.35 for conversion, then broaden with weight 0.10 plus an explicit positive second-boss-entry term before regenerating prefixes from a later incumbent.
  3. Tier 3: same harness for elite fights and Act 3 hallway spikes; plus one campaign with elite_value/penalty sigma ×3 to probe the elite-heavy basin the ES declined.

Neural combat policy: go/no-go

Verdict: NO-GO now. ES delivers 1–2pp/day of pure compute; RL must beat a 63%+ tuned beam search end-to-end. env.rs needs a rewrite anyway (reset hardcodes Ironclad; obs is 8 floats; reward pays +1 every step once act ≥ 2). Inference placement is the killer: the eval runs 10²–10³ times per decision — a net inside the loop is 50–100× slower eval, breaking outer-loop ES entirely. No GPU.

Bounded probe worth doing (2 days) after ES saturates: supervised value net V(combat state) → fight outcome from 10–50k logged runs (~800–1200-float obs: pile multisets, hand slots, orbs, player scalars, enemy intents; 2–3 layer MLP). Measure where V's ranking of beam candidates disagrees with the parametric eval on lost runs. If it sees little — RL is closed out cheaply. If a lot — distill structure, not weights: turn the disagreements into new eval features/params and let ES tune them, keeping the fast eval. Full PPO/AZ-lite: 3–4 weeks, +0–8pp with wide error bars; rank last.

Evaluation protocol

  • Binomial se at p≈0.62: 500 seeds ±4.3pp (95%), 1k ±3.0, 2k ±2.1, 10k ±0.95. Unpaired detection of +2pp needs ~9k seeds/arm; +1pp ~37k.
  • Always pair on identical cohorts and score per-seed flips (McNemar) from fixture files — 3–6× cheaper than unpaired for the same power.
  • Honest number = one 10k never-touched cohort at bake time (~3–4 min; ±0.95pp). "Honest 1k" (±3pp) cannot certify 70%.
  • Validate checkpoints on fresh per-checkpoint sources; select bakes by tail-averaged mean (see ES v4 §5). Capped seeds are bugs. No mid-campaign builds unless default-preserving.

Parallelization

Single box: evals saturate cores; the λ/seeds rebalance is the only free win. Multi-machine fan-out is the highest-leverage change and nearly free: one static binary + one params JSON + one summary line per eval — SSH task farm; K boxes = K× generations/day; async ES tolerates stragglers. One extra machine doubles compute yield and funds the A20 campaign in parallel.
