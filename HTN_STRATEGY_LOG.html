Defect A0 HTN strategy log

Branch htn-work. New experiments use paired 500-seed cohorts, release builds, and a 5,000-step cap; every cap is a loop bug, not a tolerated result. Each entry is intentionally limited to one paragraph.

References

Original 100-seed baseline

The early reference scored 8% wins and mean floor 27.95; an independent 100-seed source 1787312088712518379 scored 5% and 28.28, establishing that small cohorts have substantial variance.

Committed 15% milestone

Target-priority work reached 15% wins and mean floor 30.01 on the 100-seed source 1787314207414780189; the first proper 500-seed audit on source 1787315208666760241 measured 58/500 wins (11.60%) and mean floor 29.76 with zero caps.

Single-acquisition boss relic reset

Relic diagnostics revealed that the engine left boss offers available after selection, so every boss relic was taken twice and energy relics granted two energy; after fixing the screen lifecycle, the accepted strategy stack measured 27/500 wins (5.40%) and mean floor 28.54 on source 1787315208666760241, making all earlier absolute rates historical rather than current milestones.

Echo Form setup valuation without exposure

A fight-length-scaled Echo Form execution rule passed its tactical test, but the fixture offered Echo Form in zero of 500 fixed-cohort decks, leaving aggregate outcomes and steps unchanged; it is retained as correctness work but is not counted as a measured strategy or synergy.

Accepted strategies

Expose real shop stock and buy scored items

Replacing blind shop entry with decisions over actual affordable cards, relics, potions, and purge raised the historical cohort from 9%/27.88 to 10%/29.23 and a fresh 100-seed cohort to 7%/30.68, so the strategy was retained.

Biased Cognition triangular Focus decay

Charging the evaluator for each future point of Bias decay raised the historical cohort to 10%/29.79 and fresh source 1787312470469267103 to 14%/30.63; diagnosed seed 13 advanced from floor 31 to 47.

Exact Rebound lifecycle and next-card valuation

The engine now puts the next non-Power, non-exhausted card on top of draw and the planner values which card receives that effect; the fresh 100-seed cohort was 5%/30.28 and acquired no Rebound, so this was accepted for correctness rather than a claimed sampled win gain.

Event HP-loss guards and event specialization

Rejecting lethal event HP costs and specializing Winding Halls and Forgotten Altar raised the historical cohort to 12%/29.94 and fresh source 1787313234548553304 to 8%/30.51; seed 32 advanced from floor 44 to 46.

Combat modifier awareness

Scoring player Strength, monster Strength gain, Weak, Vulnerable, Intangible, and Bias changed the historical cohort to 14%/30.72 and fresh source 1787313410802255679 to 17%/32.94, although that run exposed one capped legality loop later fixed separately.

Combat legality and zero-progress guard

Respecting Medical Kit and Blue Candle legality and ending zero-progress status loops replayed source 1787313410802255679 at 20%/32.98 with zero caps, eliminating capped seed 8204511003183509530.

Priority targets

Prioritizing Donu, Cultists, Bronze Orbs, Torch Heads, Sentries, and Spire Spear changed replay source 1787314071256080154 to 14%/30.57 and fresh source 1787314207414780189 to 15%/30.01, including three diagnosed Donu losses converted to wins.

Delayed Self Repair healing

Valuing Self Repair before its end-of-combat heal improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 58 to 62 wins and mean floor 29.76→29.99, then fresh source 1787316226187148540 from 42 to 55 wins and 29.12→29.66; seed 6156007182303515596 became a win and seed 1883842478669890359 advanced floor 31→42.

Delayed Machine Learning draw

Valuing future extra draws kept fixed source 1787315208666760241 at 62 wins while raising mean floor 29.99→30.09, then improved fresh source 1787316864833023929 from 57 to 58 wins and 29.92→30.03; this is retained as a small, paired improvement.

Deck-aware shop relic selection

Scoring shop relics against the current orb, power, zero-cost, energy, and X-cost packages improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 62 to 64 wins, stayed 72 wins with mean 30.68→30.67 on replay source 1787317200265831039, then improved fresh source 1787318292151234368 from 57 to 59 wins and mean 29.99→30.16.

Card-package deck-building HTN

Decomposing acquisition into orb-core, Frost, Lightning, power, zero-cost, and energy tasks improved all three pre-fix paired cohorts; after boss relic acquisition was corrected, the combined card/shop planner improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 15 to 27 wins and mean 27.58→28.54, then fresh source 1787318856394675018 from 9 to 19 wins and 27.65→28.21.

Acquisition-order gates for orphan cards

Requiring Claw to have All for One, Scrape, or a Claw package, Tempest to have energy or Chemical X support, and Fusion to serve expensive-card demand improved corrected observed cohorts 27→28 and 19→23 wins, held a third at 24 wins with higher depth, and improved fresh source 1787319499337259007 from 22 to 24 wins despite mean floor 28.55→28.42.

Deck-aware Strike purge timing

Keeping early Strikes until replacement attacks exist, then increasing purge value once an orb or power engine is established, improved or held four of five corrected cohorts and raised fresh source 1787319767048101927 from 21 to 22 wins and mean floor 28.83→28.99; this replaces the rejected flat purge increases.

Bounded turn beam search

Searching card order to width 8 and depth 6 instead of greedily choosing each remaining play improved corrected fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 32 to 38 wins and mean floor 28.81→30.39, replay source 1787318856394675018 from 23 to 39 wins and 28.46→30.04, and fresh source 1787320322822582653 from 29 to 38 wins and 29.52→30.79; all runs had zero caps, while fresh-cohort runtime rose from 9 to 41 seconds.

Proactive boss potion timing

Spending Regen when injured and long-duration Focus, Cultist, Strength, Dexterity, Plated Armor, Thorns, orb, discovery, energy, draw, and upgrade potions on the opening boss turn improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 38 to 43 wins and 30.39→30.58, replay source 1787318856394675018 from 39 to 45 wins and 30.04→30.36, and fresh source 1787321283377521703 from 28 to 31 wins and 29.24→29.28, with zero caps.

Known-boss deck acquisition task

Using the revealed Act 2 boss to prioritize missing AoE for Collector, burst defense and Dark scaling for Automaton, and long-fight scaling for Champ improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 44 wins and mean floor 30.58→30.78, held replay source 1787318856394675018 at 45 wins and 30.36→30.35, and improved fresh source 1787322574873510016 from 36 to 37 wins despite mean floor 30.81→30.74, with zero caps.

Known Act 1 boss acquisition task

Extending the revealed-boss task with sustained frost and Weak for Hexaghost, block/frost for Guardian, and burst/AoE for Slime Boss improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 44 to 49 wins and mean floor 30.78→30.91, replay source 1787318856394675018 from 45 to 49 wins and 30.35→30.88, and fresh source 1787322961599519915 from 39 to 44 wins and 29.91→30.50, with zero caps.

Demote Busted Crown without reordering energy relics

Moving only Busted Crown from the neutral boss-relic score to just above Snecko Eye, Runic Dome, and Calling Bell held fixed and replay sources at 49 wins while raising mean floors 30.91→30.95 and 30.88→30.89, then improved fresh source 1787323430036114759 from 42 to 43 wins and 30.56→30.64; the fresh Spheric Guardian cap occurred with both binaries and was diagnosed separately.

Break retained-block walls

Capped seed 1792856469989672380 reached turn 1,033 because Spheric Guardian's Barricade retained more block than HP while the evaluator treated stripping block as worthless; crediting block removal only in that wall state changed fixed wins 49→52 and mean 30.95→31.11, replay wins 49→48 with mean 30.89 unchanged, and fresh wins 43→47 with mean 30.64→30.60 while the cap became a normal floor-39 loss in 980 steps.

Hold Biased Cognition for Champ's second phase

Among current Champ losses, 72 of 99 Biased Cognition decks ended with negative Focus versus 4 of 169 decks without it; preventing Biased Cognition before an unphased Champ falls below half HP improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 52 to 55 wins and mean 31.11→31.38, replay source 1787318856394675018 from 48 to 50 wins and 30.89→31.05, and fresh source 1787324441138756002 from 45 to 51 wins and 30.74→31.07, with zero caps.

Discarded strategies

Value inaccessible shops

Assigning a −25 value to shops that could not actually be reached produced 6% wins and mean floor 28.73 on the historical cohort, losing wins relative to baseline, so it was discarded before a fresh cohort.

Stronger campfire preference

Using 50/25 campfire weights and 70 near bosses gave 9%/27.88 historically and 5%/27.23 on fresh source 1787312242685583510; the fresh mean-floor regression rejected the thesis.

Aggressive Strike purge priority

Purge value 330 raised fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 62 to 71 wins and 29.99→30.17, but on fresh source 1787316573696228393 wins stayed 67 while mean floor fell 30.79→30.41, so the apparent fixed-cohort gain was rejected.

Moderate Strike purge priority

Purge value 240 raised the fixed cohort to 74 wins and mean floor 30.47, but replaying the previously observed independent source reduced wins 67→65 and mean floor 30.79→30.55, so no new random cohort was warranted.

Delayed Loop output

Explicitly pricing future front-orb triggers reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 62 to 58 wins and mean floor 29.99→29.65, so the heuristic was removed before fresh testing.

Delayed Buffer prevention

Buffer valuation moved diagnosed Automaton seed 1883842478669890359 from floor 42 to 47 and fixed wins 62→64, but lowered fixed mean floor to 29.81 and regressed fresh source 1787316759813919465 from 52 to 49 wins and 29.81→29.68.

Require two Frost sources before drafting Blizzard

The dependency rule improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 62 to 69 wins and mean floor 30.09→30.62, but fresh source 1787317200265831039 regressed from 72 to 70 wins while mean floor rose only 30.68→30.77, so the plausible Frost pairing is not yet a supported strategy.

Unbounded package bonuses for boss relics

The first joint planner fell from 62 to 59 fixed-cohort wins; decomposition identified boss relic scoring as the cause at 48 wins and mean floor 28.98 because orb-package bonuses repeatedly selected Inserter over established energy relics, so boss priorities remain on the prior conservative table.

Conservative energy-first boss relic ordering

After single-acquisition correction, prioritizing Fusion Hammer and bounded deck-aware energy choices improved two observed cohorts from 27→36 and 19→23 wins, but fresh source 1787319073388804697 regressed 24→22 wins while mean floor rose 28.22→28.38, so the old static boss table remains.

Lower the global elite-strength pathing gate

Corrected wins visited more elites than losses, but lowering the required strength from 5/7/9 to 3/4/5 by act while retaining matchup checks reduced fixed wins 32→22 and mean floor 28.81→27.80, showing that low elite exposure was mostly a symptom of weak or injured runs rather than a safe global routing opportunity.

Corrected-engine delayed Buffer prevention

Retesting Buffer after boss relic acquisition was corrected, with enough value to beat chip damage on a safe boss setup turn, reduced fixed wins 32→31 and mean floor 28.81→28.74; the future-hit heuristic still overplayed Buffer elsewhere and was removed before fresh testing.

Delay Champ's half-HP transition for burst

Penalizing shallow damage across Champ's Anger/Execute threshold held fixed source 1787315208666760241 at 38 wins while reducing Champ deaths 74→71 and raising mean floor 30.39→30.46, then improved replay source 1787318856394675018 from 39 to 40 wins and 30.04→30.19, but fresh source 1787320907338841119 regressed from 31 to 30 wins and 30.58→30.57, so the boss-specific phase heuristic was removed.

Require X-cost support before Double Energy

Across three potion-enabled cohorts Double Energy appeared in 95 decks with only three wins, but penalizing it until X-cost demand existed reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 40 wins and mean floor 30.58→30.48 with zero caps; the outcome correlation was confounded by weak decks, so the stricter gate was removed before fresh testing.

Spend scaling potions at elite openings

Extending proactive boss potion timing to elite openings reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 39 wins despite raising mean floor 30.58→30.68 with zero caps; consuming long-duration inventory before the Act boss lost more runs than the additional elite progress saved, so proactive timing remains boss-only.

Extend turn beam depth from six to eight

Keeping beam width 8 while extending the bounded sequence from six to eight remaining plays left fixed source 1787315208666760241 exactly at 43 wins and mean floor 30.58 with zero caps; five losing seeds changed floors in mixed directions but no win changed, so depth 6 remains the supported runtime/quality point.

Broad engine-critical Defect upgrade tier

Raising Fission, Biased Cognition, Seek, Consume, Machine Learning, Capacitor, Hologram, Reinforced Body, Zap, and Dualcast upgrades reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 41 wins and mean floor 30.58→30.21 with zero caps; it displaced 13 Glacier+, 10 Self Repair+, and five Defragment+ upgrades, so the bundle was decomposed rather than retained from card/outcome correlation.

Upgrade Fission and Biased Cognition below the core

Placing Fission and Biased Cognition below Defragment, Echo Form, and Glacier improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 44 wins and replay source 1787318856394675018 from 45 to 48, but fresh source 1787322079969279673 regressed 32→31 wins and mean floor 30.15→30.00 with zero caps, so even the decomposed priority was removed.

Higher Act 2 rest thresholds

Resting below 88% throughout Act 2 and below 90% before its boss reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 43 to 39 wins and mean floor 30.58→30.49 with zero caps; the observed 37-HP average boss entry was primarily downstream of weak fights and routes, and giving up more upgrades worsened survival.

Known Act 3 boss acquisition task

Adding non-Power scaling for Awakened One, efficient high-impact cards for Time Eater, and fast Focus/AoE for Donu and Deca changed 23 fixed-cohort decks but held source 1787315208666760241 at 49 wins and mean floor 30.91 with zero caps, swapping one win for one loss and moving loss floors in mixed directions, so the layer was removed before fresh testing.

Value all enemy block removal

Crediting every point of stripped enemy block at 55% of HP damage removed the diagnosed Spheric Guardian cap but reduced fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 49 to 46 wins despite raising mean floor 30.95→31.05, so ordinary resetting block remains valueless and only a retained block wall receives credit.

Value every Barricade enemy's block removal

Restricting block-removal value to all Barricade enemies improved fixed source 1787315208666760241 from 49 to 51 wins and mean floor 30.95→31.12, but replay source 1787318856394675018 regressed 49→48 wins and 30.89→30.84, so it was refined to trigger only when retained block is at least remaining HP.

Strategies that genuinely synergize

Shop visibility + reward scoring

This is a direct dependency: shop exposure supplies real purchasable choices, while deck-aware card, relic, potion, and purge scores decide among them; neither half constitutes a useful shop strategy alone.

Exact Rebound mechanics + next-card valuation

This is a direct tactical synergy: exact pile movement makes the selected repeat reliable, and the valuation chooses a high-impact card rather than merely recognizing that Rebound exists.

Focus drafting + Bias-aware orb valuation

High draft scores for Defragment, Biased Cognition, Glacier, Coolheaded, and channel sources complement combat scoring that prices Focus, orb persistence, and triangular Bias decay, forming one coherent orb engine rather than unrelated bonuses.

Modifier awareness + target priority

Strength, Weak, Vulnerable, Intangible, and enemy scaling determine the value of damage now, while target priority decides where that damage prevents the most future danger; the cumulative accepted milestone already exercises both together.

Self Repair drafting + delayed-heal play valuation

Self Repair's high reward score only helps if the combat planner spends energy to play it, and the delayed-heal value only matters when the deck acquires it; their paired 500-seed improvement makes this a genuine acquisition-to-execution synergy.

Machine Learning drafting + delayed-draw play valuation

Machine Learning's draft preference and explicit future-draw value jointly turn the card into sustained access to the existing focus, frost, and damage package; paired 500-seed results show compatibility with the accepted Self Repair and orb strategy, though not a separately proven interaction effect.

Deck-package tasks + owned and shop relics

This is a measured two-way acquisition synergy: owned relics alter which cards advance the active package, while the same deck profile alters shop relic value; shop scoring generalized independently, and adding package-aware card selection then improved all three paired cohorts on top of it.

Package enablers + gated payoff acquisition

This is a genuine acquisition-order synergy: All for One and Scrape unlock Claw, energy sources unlock Tempest and make Fusion useful, and Chemical X directly unlocks X-cost value; gating the payoff until its enabling task exists improved win counts across corrected paired cohorts.

Replacement damage + delayed Strike purge

This is a measured sequencing synergy: attacks are retained while they fill an early survival role, then become removal targets only after channel, Focus, power, and non-Strike attack packages can replace their output; corrected paired cohorts distinguish this from the rejected context-free purge bonuses.

Turn beam + order-sensitive card effects

This is a direct execution synergy: the bounded beam can compare complete card sequences while Rebound destination value and delayed Self Repair, Machine Learning, and Echo Form values make strategically useful sequences visible to it; the beam generalized across three paired cohorts on top of the accepted tactical stack, although the interaction contribution was not isolated card by card.

Shop potion scoring + proactive boss use

This is a measured acquisition-to-execution synergy: shop visibility and potion scoring create useful inventory, while early boss timing converts long-duration Focus, Strength, Dexterity, Ritual, orb, and defensive effects into value across the whole fight; proactive use improved all three paired cohorts on top of the accepted shop strategy.

Known boss + deck and relic packages

This is a measured planning synergy: the revealed Act 1 or Act 2 boss activates a matchup task for burst, AoE, block, frost, or scaling, while the existing package profile and owned relics still determine whether each candidate fits the actual deck; the Act 2 layer improved wins on two paired cohorts and held the third, then the Act 1 extension improved all three.

Busted Crown avoidance + card-package HTN

This is a genuine planning synergy: the package HTN depends on seeing enough future card rewards to assemble enablers and payoffs, while Busted Crown removes two of three reward choices; demoting only that relic preserved the established boss-relic table and improved or held wins and mean depth across all three paired cohorts.

Known Champ + delayed Biased Cognition

This is a measured acquisition-to-execution synergy: the known-boss deck task can acquire Focus and long-fight scaling for Champ, while the tactical rule withholds Biased Cognition's temporary Focus until the half-HP burst phase instead of exhausting it during the opening; the timing rule improved every paired cohort on top of the boss-aware deck stack.

Combinations not supported as synergies

Loop, Buffer, unconditional Blizzard gating, stronger campfire weights, inaccessible-shop penalties, and increased purge priority are not recommended as components merely because they sound complementary: each failed a fixed or fresh cohort, so combining them would require a new independent thesis and full two-stage evidence.
